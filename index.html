05 / WORKING PRINCIPLE

How SmartSpin Works ⚙️

1

Artisan Spins

Hand wheel turns the shaft, driving all three spindles.

2

Sensors Capture Data

Hall sensors, load cell, IR sensor and encoder read live machine activity.

3

Arduino Processes

Raw signals are converted into RPM, tension and yarn-continuity values.

4

Dashboard Updates

Processed values are pushed to the live monitoring dashboard.

5

Alerts & Feedback

System flags yarn breaks and speed drops in real time.

6

Insights for Artisan

Productivity and quality data help artisans refine their process.
